## Brand & Style

The brand visual language pays homage to the historic Mughal quadrilateral paradise garden (*Charbagh*), translating its themes of symmetry, lush botanical abundance, and artisanal craftsmanship into a sensory-rich dessert experience. Built specifically for high-throughput ordering kiosks and point-of-sale touchpoints, the interface evokes warmth, indulgence, and luxury without sacrificing operational speed or legibility.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Tactile Modernism** with subtle **Glassmorphic Surface Layers**. It avoids generic flat minimalism in favor of lush, appetizing color floods, deep warm contrast, and dimensional interactive targets calibrated for high-traffic self-service kiosks. The visual rhythm balances grand, celebratory bilingual typography with practical, fast-scanning card hierarchies, evoking the atmosphere of a heritage confectionery modernized for rapid retail.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in authentic artisanal ingredients:
- **Primary (`#D97706` - Zaffran Gold):** The heart of the brand. Used for active selection rings, primary interactive touch targets, and celebratory badges. It delivers high-visibility luminance under varied ambient kiosk lighting.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F` - Pistachio Green):** Grounding, earthy, and fresh. Applied to nutritional confirmations, dairy-free/vegan designations, category anchors, and structural navigation tabs.
- **Tertiary (`#E11D48` - Gulab Rose):** Expressive and indulgent. Reserved for flavor highlights, berry accents, promotional callouts, and seasonal alerts.
- **Neutral (`#29221D` - Roasted Cardamom Espresso):** A warm, deep brownish-black serving as high-contrast canvas text and high-importance UI boundaries.

### Canvas & Surface Tokens
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFDF7` (Whipped Vanilla Cream), providing warmth that eliminates harsh optical glare on large LCD kiosk screens.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card Backgrounds):** `#FFFFFF` with warm undertones.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Modal & Drawer Overlays):** `rgba(255, 253, 247, 0.85)` with a 16px backdrop blur.
- **POS / Thermal Receipt Accent:** Monochromatic high-contrast deep brown `#1C1613` on textured natural cream `#F7F2E8`.

## Typography

The typographical ecosystem is purposefully structured for bilingual kiosks, marrying structural Latin geometries with organic, fluid Arabic-script character forms:
- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Provides sculptural, editorial weight. Its punchy geometric structure ensures flavor titles stand out clearly from across the ordering counter.
- **Body & Touch UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly rounded counters, wide aperture, and high legibility at arm’s length on touchscreen kiosks.
- **Bilingual & Urdu Companion (Noto Serif / Arabic System Fallback):** Renders Urdu flavor descriptions (*قلفی*, *زعفران*, *پستہ*) and localized commands with literary grace, balanced line height, and authentic proportional baseline offsets. Urdu text levels must always specify +20% to +30% greater line height than Latin equivalents to prevent ascender/descender clipping.
- **Thermal POS / Receipt Styling (Space Mono):** Used on digital summary chits, checkout totals, order queuing numbers, and printout modals to evoke physical counter ticketing and kitchen routing tags.

## Layout & Spacing

Kiosk UX demands larger safe zones, thumb-accessible vertical sectors, and strict spacing discipline to prevent accidental taps:
- **Kiosk Grid (Portrait 1080x1920):** 8-column layout with `2rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Content stays contained in the reachable bottom 65% of the kiosk screen, with the top 35% reserved for high-visibility visual branding and dynamic item previews.
- **POS / Counter Terminal (Landscape 1920x1080):** 12-column layout split into a 4-column persistent cart/checkout pane and an 8-column catalog browser with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** All touchable button gaps scale using strict `space-md` (`1.25rem` / 20px) minimum padding to prevent accidental mis-taps. Section groupings use `space-xl` (`3rem`) for effortless cognitive segmenting.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is communicated through a fusion of tactile physical realism and modern frosted glass surfaces:
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Cream-tinted `#FFFDF7` planar base with subtle micro-textures.
- **Tier 1 (Catalog Tiles & Inactive Cards):** Solid `#FFFFFF` elevated with ambient downward shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(41, 34, 29, 0.06)`, bounded by a 1px soft border in `rgba(41, 34, 29, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Selected Cards & Popovers):** Elevated with an enriched warm amber glow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.18)` and an active 2px solid `#D97706` frame.
- **Tier 3 (Glassmorphic Modals & Scoop Customizers):** Background overlay `rgba(41, 34, 29, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. The customizer sheet utilizes `rgba(255, 253, 247, 0.92)` surface fill, a top-specular inner highlight `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, and deep elevation `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(41, 34, 29, 0.25)`.
- **Thermal Monospace Slips:** Flat, paper-like surface `#F7F2E8` rendered with crisp jagged edge masks top/bottom, zero blur shadow, and a 1px dashed divider border simulating perforated paper edges.

## Shapes

The design uses **Level 2 (Rounded)** architecture to echo the rounded scoops of gelato and kulfi molds:
- **Base Components:** Standard buttons, chips, and input fields feature `0.5rem` (8px) base radii.
- **Product Tiles & Kiosk Containers:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) corners to provide soft containment for vibrant product photography.
- **Floating Modals & Scoop Selectors:** Feature `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for approachable consumer friendliness.
- **Steppers & Quantity Selectors:** Kept fully circular (`border-radius: 9999px`) to immediately communicate interactive touch suitability.

## Components

### 1. Kiosk Flavor Cards
- Vertical layout comprising a top visual image container (1:1 ratio with soft pistachio or rose gradient underlay), bilingual title stack (`headline-sm` in Latin above `urdu-body` in Nastaliq/Arabic script), ingredient badge pills, and a fixed bottom pricing/selection row.
- **Selected State:** Shifts background from neutral white to a delicate cream saffron tint (`#FFFBEB`), applies a 2px stroke of `#D97706`, and presents a circular checkmark badge anchored in the top-right corner.
- **Touch Target:** Minimum height 180px, width 100% of grid column span.

### 2. Buttons & Touch Selectors
- **Primary Kiosk CTA ("Add to Cup", "Proceed to Pay"):** Minimum height 64px. Deep gold background (`#D97706`) with high-contrast text (`#FFFFFF`). Subtle pressed state scales down slightly (`scale(0.98)`) with a warm outer glow.
- **Secondary Buttons ("Customize Toppings", "Cancel"):** Pistachio-tinted outlines (`2px solid #2D6A4F`) on transparent or white backgrounds with `#2D6A4F` text.
- **Language Switcher (EN | اردو):** Segmented pill toggle docked in the kiosk top header with high-contrast label typography.

### 3. Scoop & Portion Builder (Customizer Modal)
- Centered glassmorphic card presenting an interactive 3D/layered ice cream visual.
- Selection rows for Base (Waffle Cone, Saffron Brioche, Artisan Cup) followed by Scoops and Exotic Toppings (Crushed Pistachio, Silver Vark, Edible Rose Petals).
- Quantity controls: Large 48px circular buttons with oversized `+` and `−` symbols ensuring error-free tapping.

### 4. Chips & Dietary Tags
- Compact 36px-height capsules with 8px horizontal padding.
- `Vegan / Dairy-Free`: Soft sage fill (`rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.12)`) with `#2D6A4F` text.
- `Signature / Chef's Pick`: Rosy tint (`rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.1)`) with `#E11D48` text.

### 5. Input Fields (Tips, Phone Loyalty, Custom Notes)
- 56px minimum touch height with large 18px body font.
- Default border: 1.5px `#E5E0D8`. Focused state: 2px `#D97706` with an ambient amber focus ring (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`).

### 6. POS Monospace Receipt / Digital Ticket
- Styled with `receipt-mono` typography on `#F7F2E8`.
- Contains order ticket number in massive mono figures, bilingual itemized flavor breakdowns, tax breakdown, and dashed line perforations (`border-style: dashed; border-color: rgba(41, 34, 29, 0.2)`).
- Used on the final confirmation screen and printed directly onto kitchen thermal tape.
